# Doopi & Friends, Season 2: The Whispering Horn

Storyline proposal for Sridhar's review. No scripts yet. 24 episodes of about 5 minutes each,
same format as season 1: the `doopi_intro` template, a short "Last time..." recap, one main
adventure, and a "To be continued..." hook at the end.

## Where season 1 left off

- The seven crystals are home, the valley has its colours back and Nani Nimbu is awake.
- Peepa is back to his normal size, sitting in a little cage at the edge of the square,
  tapping his foot to the music and *almost* smiling.
- The Gloom Horn "rolled away". Nobody picked it up.
- The Rainbow Banyan cracked when Giant Peepa hit the valley (ep21). Nobody has fixed the crack.
- Tony, Kony, Thunda, Ghoomu, Tara, Mallu Uncle and even Madam Mirchi ended on the good side.

## 1. Premise and arc

**Premise.** The Gloom Horn was never just a horn. Inside it lives **the Hush**, a small purple
whisper that finds people with a big feeling (jealousy, fear, loneliness, wanting to win) and
makes that feeling bigger, until a little grey rain cloud sits over their head and they stop
being themselves. Long ago the Hush whispered to a lonely boy with horns, and he became Peepa.
Now it is loose again, hopping from person to person, and heading home to the **Echo Cave**
beyond the valley, where it can grow into a storm.

**The season's question.** Season 1 asked "how do you fight a villain?" and answered "together".
Season 2 asks "how do you help someone who is having a big feeling?" The answer is not a punch.
Doopi finds out in episode 2 that the Jalebi Spin makes a grey cloud *bigger*. What makes it
smaller is noticing, asking, listening and saying the feeling out loud. Each episode, one person
gets a cloud; the friends help them name the feeling; the cloud pops and the Hush zips away.

**Doopi's arc.** In season 1 Doopi learned to be a hero. In season 2 he learns to be a friend
to someone nobody trusts. He remembers being "clumsy Doopi" and stands up for Peepa first, but
he also has to stop treating every problem as a fight, and he gets his own cloud (episode 8,
wanting to win as "the hero").

**Peepa's arc.** The real story of the season. Out of the cage on a second chance, living with
Nani as her helper. Grumpy, proud, bad at saying sorry, secretly musical. The village does not
trust him, and in the middle of the season the friends stop trusting him too. In the end the Hush
offers him the Horn and his old size back, and he chooses to be small and have friends. The horn
becomes a flute, and Peepa plays the song that heals the Banyan.

**The payoff.** Everyone the friends help during the season (Chintu, Gappu, Ghadi Dada, Zippy,
Bijli and others) comes back in the finale. The people you listened to become the choir that
sings the Hush to sleep. Each helped person leaves a small glowing music note when their cloud
pops; the notes are a visual thread, not a fetch quest (nobody has to collect them).

**Shape of the season.**

| Episodes | Part | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | A second chance (the Hush wakes up in Tokyo) | Tokyo village, Banyan hill, Nani's hut |
| 5–6 | On the road | Road, Whispering Forest |

- A small grey rain-cloud fx that can sit over one character's head and grow or shrink.

## 3. Episodes

Each episode carries one lesson or feeling. Every cloud is temporary and pops when the feeling
is named.

### Part 1: A second chance

**1. Peepa's First Day**
The morning after the festival, Nani lets Peepa out of the cage: "Everyone deserves a second
chance. Even you." He has to help at the jalebi stall and sweep the square, but the villagers
hide their children and he breaks everything he touches. When the kids laugh at him, Doopi, who
remembers being "trippy Doopi", helps him up. That night Becky digs at the spot on Banyan hill
where the Gloom Horn rolled: it is gone, and a tiny voice giggles "hushhh...".
*Lesson: everyone deserves a second chance.*

**2. The Little Grey Cloud**
At the kite festival, new boy Gappu's kite flies higher than Chintu's, and a little grey cloud
appears over Chintu's head. The cloud grows and rains on the whole festival. Doopi tries a
Jalebi Spin on it and it doubles in size. Peepa goes quiet and says, "That's the Hush. It talked
to me once." Doomi simply asks Chintu what is wrong; he admits he is jealous, and the cloud pops
into a glowing music note while a violet puff zips away.
*Lesson: name the feeling. It's okay to feel jealous; it's not okay to be mean about it.*

**3. Gappu's Giant Stories**
Gappu wants friends, so he tells giant stories: he fought a tiger, he has a flying bicycle. The
Hush makes his stories appear as big grey pretend-monsters that chase the village. The friends
stop them by asking Gappu to tell one true thing about himself ("I'm scared nobody will like
me"). Peepa admits he tells fibs too. At sunset a stranger with a flute walks into the square.
*Lesson: you don't need big stories. The real you is enough.*

**4. Bansuri Baba's Song**
Bansuri Baba tells the Horn's secret: it was once a flute that played the valley's song, until
the Hush crept inside and turned it into the Gloom Horn. Now the Hush is heading home to the Echo
Cave, where it can grow into a storm. The friends will follow it, with Ghoomu to carry the bags.
Peepa asks to come; the villagers say no; Nani says yes, and gives him one job: "Look after them."
*Lesson: trust is built in small steps.*

### Part 2: On the road

**5. Mooka and the Last Jalebi**
On the road, Mooka has one jalebi left and Peepa is hungry. Mooka, who always shares, hides it,
and a grey cloud appears over the sweetest friend of all. The friends are amazed; Becky growls at
the cloud. In the end, Peepa offers Mooka his own lunch first, and Mooka breaks the jalebi into
seven pieces.
*Lesson: anyone can have a grumpy day; sharing makes the feeling smaller.*

**6. Dony and the Dark**
Camp night in the Whispering Forest, with Tara visiting. Dony, the joker, keeps finding excuses
not to go past the firelight, and the Hush finds his secret: he is scared of the dark. His cloud
blows out the fire. Doopi doesn't laugh; he holds his hand, and Tara shows them that the forest
glows with fireflies once your eyes get used to the dark.
*Lesson: being brave doesn't mean not being scared.*

### Part 3: The big city

**7. Jhilmil City**
The Hush's trail leads to Jhilmil City: tall buildings, honking, and nobody says hello. Doopi
wanders off after a violet puff and gets lost in the crowd. He is too proud to ask for help until
Zippy, a fast-talking tiffin courier on roller skates, stops and asks him. Becky follows his
headband smell to bring the team back together.
*Lesson: it's smart, not silly, to ask for help.*

**8. The Rooftop Race**
Zippy challenges "the famous hero" to a rooftop tiffin-delivery race. The Hush whispers to Doopi
that heroes always win, and he takes a shortcut he knows is against the rules; a tiffin falls and
somebody's lunch is ruined. With a cloud over his own head, Doopi owns up and shares his lunch
with the hungry worker. Zippy decides she likes this hero.
*Lesson: winning is not the most important thing. Playing fair is.*

**9. The Clock That Stopped**
All the clocks in Jhilmil City stop, and the whole city freezes in a grey hush. At the top of the
clock tower, old Ghadi Dada sits under a huge cloud. Nobody has visited him in years. The friends
don't fight; they stay, listen to his stories and play a game of carrom, and the clock starts
ticking again.
*Lesson: loneliness gets smaller when someone sits with you.*

**10. Doomi's Robot**
Doomi builds a little robot, Tinku, out of city scrap, with a radar to find clouds. It fails,
and fails again, and the Hush gives Doomi a cloud: "Clever girls don't make mistakes."
Ghoomu, who was once a "mistake" himself, shows her his dented plates. On the eleventh try Tinku
beeps a happy tune and points: the Hush has left the city for the desert.
*Lesson: mistakes are how we learn.*

### Part 4: Far lands, and the trust crack

**11. Bijli of the Sands**
In the Hot Sands, the water runs out and Mooka sees a jalebi-shop mirage. The only well is
guarded by Bijli, a tiny girl with the loudest voice in the world, and she won't share because
travellers once tricked her. ("THUNDA? My brother? He's the SMALL one.") The friends fix her
broken well rope first, without asking for anything, and her cloud pops.
*Lesson: kindness without asking for anything back builds trust.*

**12. The Night of Stars**
A quiet episode. Around the campfire, each friend tells one thing they are scared of. Peepa,
last, says: "That nobody will ever really like me." In the night, the Hush rises as a sandstorm
shadow and whispers only to Peepa: "They'll never trust you. Take me back and be big again."
In the morning, Peepa is gone, and so is the Hush's trail.
*Lesson: saying a fear out loud makes it smaller. (Cliffhanger: where is Peepa?)*

**13. Where Is Peepa?**
The team splits. Dony and Mooka think Peepa tricked them all along; Doopi wants to believe him.
Tinku's radar shows two signals, the Hush and Peepa chasing it. They find Peepa trying to catch
the Hush alone: "It's my mess. I wanted to fix it by myself." Doopi laughs gently: "I tried that
once. It doesn't work." Dony says sorry for blaming him.
*Lesson: ask before you blame.*

**14. Pick Me Last**
In Khel Arena, a sports town, the Hush makes everyone a sore loser and every game ends in a sulk.
When the friends choose teams for the town's big tug-of-war, Peepa stands at the back expecting
to be picked last, like when he was little. Doopi picks him first. Peepa's team loses, and
everyone shakes hands anyway, and the arena's clouds pop one by one.
*Lesson: be a good sport, and make room for everyone.*

### Part 5: To the Echo Cave

**15. Echo Mountain**
On Echo Mountain, whatever you shout comes back, and the Hush makes the echoes say the mean
things each friend thinks about themselves ("Clumsy!" "Too small!" "Horn-head!"). Doopi teaches
everyone to shout something kind about themselves instead, and the mountain echoes it back
louder. Peepa's turn is the hardest: "I'm... getting better!"
*Lesson: talk to yourself the way you'd talk to a friend.*

**16. Ghoomu's Big Heart**
Tinku can do clever things Ghoomu can't, and Ghoomu starts to feel too big, too slow and in the
way. The Hush gives him a cloud and he powers down in the snow. At the frozen river, only someone
big and strong can carry everyone across, and Tinku climbs up on Ghoomu's shoulder and beeps,
"You're my hero."
*Lesson: everyone has their own special gifts.*

**17. Becky's Thunder Night**
A mountain thunderstorm, and brave Becky, who dived into Lotus Lake for Doopi, is terrified of
thunder. The Hush makes her cloud the biggest yet, and she hides in a crack in the rocks.
Nobody can coax her out until Peepa sits quietly and plays a soft tune on a leaf whistle. The
first time anyone hears Peepa make music.
*Lesson: be gentle with a scared friend; you don't have to fix it, just be there.*

**18. Mooka's Jar**
Using Tinku's radar, the friends finally trap the Hush in a glass jar. While everyone sleeps,
the Hush sweet-talks Mooka ("just a peek, little jelly"), Mooka opens the lid, and it escapes.
Mooka hides what he did, and the guilt grows into a cloud. When he finally owns up, nobody is
cross, and Bansuri Baba's words make sense: "You can't keep a feeling in a jar."
*Lesson: owning up to a mistake feels better than hiding it.*

**19. The Door of True Words**
The Echo Cave's stone door opens only for someone who says a true feeling out loud. One by one,
the friends say theirs and walk in. When Peepa steps up, the door stays shut: the cave remembers
the boy who came here angry. He tries again, and this time he says he is sorry. The door
creaks open.
*Lesson: being honest about how you feel, even when it's hard.*

**20. The Little Boy in the Cave**
Deep inside, the crystal walls show echoes of the past: little Peepa, alone, and the first time
the Hush spoke to him. This time grown-up Peepa kneels in front of the echo and says what nobody
said back then: "You can play with us." The Hush, which has grown fat on every feeling it has
gathered, pulls the Horn out of the crystals and blows away as a huge storm, straight toward
Tokyo.
*Lesson: you can be kind to the little you who was hurt. (Cliffhanger: the storm heads home.)*

### Part 6: The Hush Storm and the Banyan Song

**21. The Hush Storm**
Ghoomu, Zippy and Bijli race the friends home. In Tokyo, the storm has given everyone a cloud:
nobody talks, nobody plays, and even Mayor Motumal can't get a single word out. In the panic Doopi
forgets everything he has learned and throws a Rainbow Punch at the storm, which only makes it
bigger. Nani holds his hands: "Doopi. What worked every single time?"
*Lesson: when things feel too big, slow down and remember what works.*

**22. Every Cloud**
The friends go house to house and do the season's work in fast-forward: they ask, they listen,
they help. Kony helps Tony, Gappu tells the truth, Chintu shares his kite. Cloud after cloud pops
into music notes. But the storm's heart sits on the cracked Banyan, holding the Gloom Horn, and it
wants only Peepa. Peepa walks up the hill alone, on purpose this time, and the Hush offers him
the Horn: "Be big again. Be strong. Make them sorry."
*Lesson: helping one person at a time adds up. (Cliffhanger: Peepa takes the Horn.)*

**23. Peepa's Choice**
Peepa lifts the Gloom Horn to his lips... and hands it to Doopi: "I don't want to be big. I want
to be your friend." The angry storm wraps Peepa in grey (he is safe, just still and quiet).
Bansuri Baba calls out: the Horn doesn't need a blast, it needs a song. And up the hill come
all the people the friends helped this season, from Chintu and Ghadi Dada to Zippy and Bijli,
with Tara, Mallu Uncle, Thunda and Madam Mirchi.
*Lesson: choosing kindness when it's hard is the bravest thing.*

**24. The Banyan Song**
The whole valley hums Bansuri Baba's song, Doopi presses the Horn into Peepa's grey hands, and
the music wakes him. Peepa plays: the grey shell of the Horn cracks away and it becomes a golden
bamboo flute. The storm shrinks into one tiny violet puff, a feeling that only wanted to be
heard, and it curls up to sleep in the Banyan. The crack in the Banyan heals.
At the first rain of the monsoon, Tokyo holds a Rain Festival. The village kids walk up to Peepa
and ask, "Can you play with us?" He trips over his own feet in the first game, and laughs.
*Lesson: when a feeling is heard, it can finally rest.*

## 4. How the season ends

- The Gloom Horn is a flute again: the **Banyan Flute**, and Peepa is its player.
- The Hush is not destroyed. It sleeps inside the healed Banyan as a tiny violet puff, and the
  village knows what to do if a little grey cloud ever shows up: ask, listen, name the feeling.
- Peepa lives with Nani, is Kony's best friend, plays at every festival and is still bad at
  saying sorry, but better.
- Zippy, Bijli and Tinku have friends in Tokyo; Tinku stays with Doomi.
- Mayor Motumal finally finishes a speech, and it is one sentence long.
- **Closing image:** at the Rain Festival, Peepa and Doopi trip over each other in the same game,
  and both laugh. It mirrors ep1 of season 1 ("The Boy Who Tripped") and ep20 ("Can I play too?").
- **Optional season 3 hook (one line, can be cut):** a paper boat floats down the monsoon stream
  into the square, with a drawing of a faraway island and the words "Help us, Doopi."

## Notes for scripting (if approved)

- Season 1 plots this avoids: crystal-collecting, kidnapping and rescue, a giant final battle with
  a power blast, a robot villain, a shadow copy, a Grey Moon countdown. The only quest here is
  following the Hush, and no episode is solved by a fight.
- Fighting still appears (it's part of the fun), but it never works on a cloud, and the series'
  moves (Jalebi Spin, Rainbow Punch, Banyan Root Stance) are used for chases, games and rescues.
- Recurring gags to keep: Motumal's unfinished speech ("In the year of the big mango..."),
  Mooka's sneezes and "Wait for meee!", Doopi tripping, Becky growling at danger.
- Each cloud episode follows the same easy beat for small viewers: big feeling, grey cloud,
  fighting makes it worse, someone asks "what's wrong?", the feeling is named, the cloud pops into
  a music note.
- Camera and pace: use ep01's approved pace block (glide 1.2, calm cuts), as for season 1.
