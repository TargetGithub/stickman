# Baaaana words

The shared speech list for every Baaaana video (BIBLE.md section 8). Bubbles hold one or two of these, never a
sentence. The engine's babble voice (`meta.speech` babble, the default) speaks them in each lead's own pitch
and timbre; real words land as punchlines.

## Baaaana sounds (anyone)

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| Baaaana! | hello, thank you, sorry, wow, look out (it depends on the face) |
| Ooh! / Oooooh | impressed (with the `ooh` sound) |
| Pa-pa-poo! | a fanfare, ta-da |
| Bee-doo bee-doo | an alarm, a siren, hurry |
| Hee-hee! | a giggle (with the `giggle` sound) |
| Pthbbbt | a raspberry (with the `raspberry` sound) |
| Uh-oh | something is about to go wrong |
| Yum-yum-baaa! | food! |
| Tada-baaa! | a gadget reveal |

## Real words from many languages (punchlines)

Use about one per scene, at the end of a gag, so everyone in the world gets a word they know.

| Meaning | Words |
|---|---|
| hello | namaste, hola, bonjour, ciao, salaam, vanakkam, konnichiwa |
| thank you | gracias, merci, danke, arigato, shukriya, xie xie, nandri |
| sorry | pardon, sumimasen, maaf |
| wow | wah, olé, sugoi |
| yummy | delicioso, miam, oishii |
| goodnight | buenas noches, bonne nuit, oyasumi, shubh ratri |
| one, two, three | uno dos tres, un deux trois, ek do teen, ichi ni san |
| cheers | salud, santé, prost, kanpai |

## Sounds to pair (catalog `effects/sfx.json`)

`giggle`, `giggle_crowd`, `ooh`, `raspberry`, `slide_whistle`, `squeak`, `sprout_spring`, `toot` (trumpet),
`ding` (triangle), plus the older `boing`, `bonk`, `pop`, `gasp`, `laugh_small`. The `bouncy` music mood is the
Baaaana theme; `lullaby` for sleepy scenes.
