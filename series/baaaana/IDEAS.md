# Baaaana: idea bank

Status: **draft** (2026-10-24). Twelve standalone videos of about 20 minutes each. Pick any one; none depends on
another. Format (BIBLE.md section 7): a cold open (about 1 min), the title card, four segments of 4 to 5 minutes on
one theme, a 30-second interlude after segment 2, and a closing song (1 to 2 min). Every segment follows the
shape: Bobo's plan, three escalating tries (Zizi's gadget, the crowd copying), a big wobble (Tutu holds it
together), a happy surprise (Mooka), a giggle and a look to camera.

Each idea lists its places, the engine features it shows off, and its two layers of humour (what kids laugh at,
what grown-ups laugh at). Words come from WORDS.md; sounds and the `bouncy` theme from task 10.

Feature shorthand: **road** (walking toward or away from the camera, ENGINE_V3_SPEC 5.10), **climb** (platforms,
5.11), **lift** (the camera rises, 5.12), **crowd** (copy_leader, conga, sync_dance, wave, pile_on,
scatter_giggle), **games** (band_play, group_photo, bubble_party, picnic, garden_planting, conga_line,
pillow_fight, sports_day_race, tug_of_war, human_pyramid).

---

## 1. Picnic Day (recommended first video)

The whole village packs one picnic basket for a day out on the hill.
- **Cold open:** Bobo blows the whistle for a line-up; fifty Baaaanas line up perfectly, then one sneezes and the whole line scatters giggling (scatter_giggle).
- **Seg 1, The Basket:** Bobo's checklist; Zizi's "basket shrinker" gadget; the crowd copies Bobo packing, badly (copy_leader). Tutu eats the sandwiches as they are packed. Mooka finds the basket was empty all along and has packed only flowers.
- **Seg 2, The Road:** the four walk down the road to the hill, toward the camera (road), trees streaming past; a conga line forms behind them (conga); they walk away into the distance, then run back for the forgotten basket.
- *Interlude:* a stadium wave across the meadow (wave).
- **Seg 3, The Blanket:** the picnic (picnic): a bee, a scatter, the cake on the yoga mat, a group photo where someone always blinks (group_photo).
- **Seg 4, The Hill:** Zizi climbs the lookout tower to spot the best view (climb, lift); everyone piles on to see (pile_on); human pyramid wobbles (human_pyramid).
- **Closing song:** sunset band on the hill (band_play, sync_dance).
- **Kids:** sneezes, bees, the pyramid falling. **Grown-ups:** packing for a family day out, the group photo, the checklist.

## 2. The Big Wedding

Two crowd Baaaanas are getting married; the four leads are in charge of everything.
- Segments: the invitations (a delivery run down the road), the cake (Tutu's taste-testing), the photos (group_photo, the camera_tripod gag), the first dance (sync_dance turns into a conga).
- **Grown-ups:** the wedding planner (Bobo), speeches that go on too long, the photographer.

## 3. Sports Day

The meadow holds its sports day.
- Segments: warm-up (yoga mat, everyone copying the coach), the race (sports_day_race with a slide-whistle fall), tug of war (tug_of_war, both teams on their bottoms), the medal ceremony on the garden wall podium (climb).
- **Grown-ups:** the over-serious parent coach, the participation trophy.

## 4. Snow Day

The first snow on the meadow (snow_mountain theme, weather snow).
- Segments: getting dressed (too many scarves), the snowball fight (pillow_fight with snowballs), building a snow-Baaaana (human_pyramid as a snowman), sledding down the hill toward the camera (road).
- **Grown-ups:** the school's snow day closure, the hot chocolate queue.

## 5. The Talent Show

Every Baaaana has a talent; Bobo is the judge.
- Segments: auditions (the crowd copies each act, copy_leader), Zizi's magic act going wrong, Tutu's dance, Mooka sleeps through the final and wins.
- **Grown-ups:** TV talent show parody, the judges' faces.

## 6. Moving House

Tutu moves to a new mushroom house across the meadow.
- Segments: packing boxes, carrying the sofa (a conga line under it), the road trip with everything (road, the world streaming past), unpacking where nothing fits.
- **Grown-ups:** flat-pack instructions, the friend who "helps".

## 7. The Garden Contest

Who grows the biggest vegetable?
- Segments: digging (garden_planting), Zizi's growth machine, the night watch on the garden wall (climb at night), the judging (a giant pumpkin rolls away down the road).
- **Grown-ups:** competitive neighbours, the allotment committee.

## 8. Birthday Surprise

It is Mooka's birthday, and Mooka must not find out.
- Segments: hiding the cake, decorating (party hats on everyone), keeping Mooka busy (a walk away down the road), the surprise that goes off three times.
- **Grown-ups:** organising a surprise party, the group chat.

## 9. The Bank Queue

The meadow gets a bank. Nobody knows how queues work.
- Segments: the queue (conga turns into a queue), the form, the lost pen, the vault (a ladder, climb, lift).
- **Grown-ups:** queueing, paperwork, "please take a number".

## 10. Yoga Class

Bobo teaches yoga. The crowd copies everything (copy_leader), including the sneeze.
- Segments: breathing, the poses (human_pyramid by accident), the balance class on the garden wall (climb), the nap at the end where Mooka snores.
- **Grown-ups:** fitness class parody, the instructor's calm voice.

## 11. Camping Trip

The four go camping by the lake (lotus_lake theme).
- Segments: the hike (road, away into the distance), the tent, the campfire song (band_play), the night-time scare that is only a frog (scatter_giggle).
- **Grown-ups:** dad and the tent instructions, the "short cut".

## 12. The Music Festival

The meadow hosts a music festival.
- Segments: building the stage (climb, lift), the sound check (toot, ding), the crowd surfing Baaaana (pile_on, wave), the big finale (sync_dance with everyone).
- **Grown-ups:** festival life, the portable toilet queue (gentle), the one dancing friend.

---

## Choosing

**Picnic Day** is the best first video: it uses nearly every new feature once (road, climb and lift, every crowd behaviour, picnic, photo, pyramid, band), it needs only the meadow and a road, and its story is simple enough to hold 20 minutes without dialogue.
