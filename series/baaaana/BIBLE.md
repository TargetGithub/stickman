# Baaaana: channel bible

Status: **draft for Sridhar's review** (2026-10-23). Plan and task list: [PLAN.md](PLAN.md).

## 1. The show in one line

A meadow full of small, round, green Baaaanas who turn every everyday thing (a picnic, a queue, a photo, a birthday) into happy chaos, and always end up dancing.

- **Channel:** "Baaaana", separate from Doopi & Friends. No crossovers, no shared videos.
- **Not a series:** every video is unique and independent. No running story, no numbering, no seasons; a viewer can start with any video. Only the cast, the world and the format carry over.
- **Audience:** everyone, from small kids to grandparents. Watchable with the sound off, in any country.
- **Length:** about 20 minutes per video (section 7).
- **Tone:** warm, silly, kind. Nobody is mean for long, nobody gets hurt, and every mess ends in a giggle.

## 2. The world

**Baaaana Meadow** is a sunny hill of tall grass, a stream, a big old tree, and a village of round mushroom-cap huts. The Baaaanas tend a vegetable garden, keep bees, and love music, snacks and sunshine.

The Baaaanas also go out into the wider world (a town, the beach, the snow, a city, a fair). Humans there are background extras who stay puzzled, charmed, or quietly amused. The Baaaanas are never anyone's servants or henchmen; they help because they want to.

Places reused from the catalog: city, park, beach, snow mountain, market and the other Doopi themes, re-dressed. New place: `baaaana_meadow`.

## 3. What a Baaaana is

- A round jelly-bean body (`buddy` body plan), **mint-green family**, with a **lavender belly patch**.
- Two big round eyes with pupils that dart around and look at the camera.
- A **sprout on top** instead of hair. It bounces when they walk, perks up when they're happy and droops when they're sad.
- **Orange mitten hands**, stubby legs, and a squishy body that squashes when they land and wobbles when they giggle.
- They love berries, jalebis, dumplings and anything shiny. They hate going to bed and loud thunder.
- They babble. One word is all a Baaaana needs: "Baaaana!" can mean hello, thank you, sorry, wow, or "look out".

### Originality rules (always)

| Never | Instead |
|---|---|
| yellow skin | mint, teal-mint, lime-mint, sea-green |
| goggles | bare big eyes (sunglasses only as a one-off gag) |
| overalls, dungarees, blue denim | bare body; accessories only (hat, backpack, scarf) |
| one central eye | always two eyes |
| bananas as the favourite food | berries, jalebis, dumplings |
| serving a villain or "evil master" | a free village of gardeners and friends |
| the word "minion" anywhere | "Baaaana" |

## 4. The four leads

### Mooka (`bn_mooka`): the little one
- **Look:** small and round (scale 0.85), mint `#7fe8c4`, a single leaf sprout, pink cheeks. Starts as a copy of Doopi's Mooka; Doopi's preset stays untouched.
- **Personality:** innocent like a small child. Copies whatever the others do (badly), giggles at everything, eats anything shiny, falls asleep at the worst moment, and now and then says exactly the wise thing by accident.
- **Running gags:** the giggle that spreads to the whole crowd; falling asleep mid-plan; hugging things that shouldn't be hugged (a cactus, a fridge, a confused cow).
- **Voice:** pitch 1.9, speed 1.3, squeaky. Babble: "Mooka!", "Ooh!", "Bana-na-na!"

### Bobo (`bobo`): the planner
- **Look:** tall and thin (scale 1.05, narrow torso), teal-mint, twin-leaf sprout, a clipboard and a whistle.
- **Personality:** the worried grown-up of the group. Makes big plans, gives speeches, blows the whistle, counts everyone twice. The plans always go wrong; Bobo always forgives everyone.
- **Running gags:** the clipboard plan that gets longer every scene; the whistle nobody obeys; a long proud sigh when something finally works. This is the character parents will see themselves in.
- **Voice:** pitch 1.5, speed 1.0, soft. Babble: "Baaa-na. Baaa-na!", "Okey-dokey!", "Uno, dos, tres!"

### Tutu (`tutu`): the big heart
- **Look:** wide and chubby (scale 0.95, wide torso), lime-mint, a flower-bud sprout that blooms when Tutu is happy.
- **Personality:** strongest and gentlest. Loves food, cooking and dancing. Cries happy tears at weddings, sunsets and good dumplings. Lifts heavy things without noticing.
- **Running gags:** sneaking a snack in every scene; the flower sprout blooming at the wrong moment; carrying the whole Baaaana tower when it falls.
- **Voice:** pitch 1.3, speed 0.9, soft. Babble: "Yum-yum-baaa!", "Aww!", "Namaste!"

### Zizi (`zizi`): the mischief maker
- **Look:** tiny and quick (scale 0.7), sea-green, a curly-vine sprout, a mini backpack full of gadgets.
- **Personality:** cheeky, curious, never still. Builds gadgets from junk (a spoon catapult, a balloon jetpack, a bubble machine) that work brilliantly once and then go wild.
- **Running gags:** "one small button" that causes the whole video; peeking out of odd places (a teapot, a hat, a mailbox); a raspberry blown at Bobo's whistle.
- **Voice:** pitch 2.0, speed 1.4, squeaky. Babble: "Zizi-zi!", "Ta-daaa!", "Oopsie!"

### How they work together

Bobo plans, Zizi breaks the plan, Tutu saves everyone (often by accident, by lifting something), and Mooka finds the happy ending because he wasn't paying attention. That loop shapes every segment.

## 5. The crowd

- The rest of the village: 20 to 50 Baaaanas, built from the `baaaana_crowd` preset with seeded variety (shade, height 0.65-1.0, sprout kind, belly shade). The same seed always gives the same crowd.
- The crowd acts as one character with a few habits:
  - **Copy the leader:** one Baaaana does something; all the others copy it a beat late, some the wrong way round.
  - **Wave:** the stadium wave, for no reason, at any moment.
  - **Pile-on:** a hug or a cheer that becomes a heap.
  - **Scatter-giggle:** a fright (a bee, a hiccup, a balloon popping), everyone runs, then they all laugh.
- Two or three crowd Baaaanas can have small recurring bits (one who always arrives late, one who loves the triangle, one who is always asleep), but no names until the audience wants them.

## 6. Humour guide

**Two layers in every scene.** Kids laugh at the slip, the bonk, the mess. Adults laugh at the situation: the queue, the instruction manual, the group photo where someone always blinks, the speech that goes on too long.

**Situations that work for everyone:** a bank or post-office queue, a yoga class, a wedding, a job interview, flat-pack furniture, a cooking show, a hospital waiting room (visiting, never hurt), the first day at school, a family road trip, a birthday surprise, moving house, a talent show, a sports day, a museum with a "do not touch" sign.

**Gag tools:**
- **Rule of three:** it works, it works, it goes wildly wrong.
- **Escalation through the crowd:** one Baaaana, then ten, then all of them.
- **The look to camera:** a Baaaana stops and looks at the viewer after something silly.
- **Sound as a joke:** a squeak, a boing, a single "Baaaana?" in a silent room.
- **Music timing:** gags land on the beat; every video ends in a song or dance.

**Never:** blood or gore, real injury, cruelty, mocking any person or group, scary villains, rude or toilet jokes beyond a gentle burp, the words "die", "kill" or "dead". A fall ends in a bounce and a giggle. Someone who is upset gets a hug before the segment ends.

## 7. Video format (about 20 minutes)

| Part | Length | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | about 1 min | a sight gag with the crowd before the title |
| Title card | about 10 s | "Baaaana" logo jingle |
| Segment 1 | 4-5 min | a complete mini-story |
| Segment 2 | 4-5 min | a complete mini-story |
| Interlude | about 30 s | a crowd gag or a mini dance |
| Segment 3 | 4-5 min | a complete mini-story |
| Segment 4 | 4-5 min | a complete mini-story |
| Closing song | 1-2 min | all four leads and the crowd dance; credits |

- Each video has a **theme** (for example "Picnic Day", "The Big Wedding", "Snow Day"), and its four segments are variations on that theme, so an video feels like one day out.
- Each segment stands alone, so it doubles as a chapter in the long video and as source material for Shorts.
- Segment shape: a small goal (Bobo's plan), three attempts that escalate (Zizi's gadget, the crowd copying), a big wobble (Tutu holds it together), a happy surprise (Mooka), a giggle and a look to camera.
- Pace: the calm Doopi pace block (slow glides, few cuts, a steady frame during gags) unless an video needs something else.
- Renders: review at 720p/1080p 30 fps; finals at 4K 60 fps.

## 8. Speech and sound

- **Bubbles:** one or two babble words, or a picture in a thought bubble (a dumpling, a heart, a lightning bolt). No sentences.
- **Real words:** about 30 friendly words from many languages, used as a joke or a punchline: hello, thank you, sorry, wow, yummy, goodnight, one-two-three ("namaste", "gracias", "merci", "danke", "arigato", "ciao", "shukriya", "vanakkam", "salaam", "xie xie"...). Kept in one shared list so the four leads stay consistent.
- **SFX:** giggles (single and crowd), "ooh", a gasp, a raspberry, boing, squeak, pop, slide whistle, a sprout "spring".
- **Music:** a bouncy main theme, a lullaby version for sleepy scenes, and one new song per video for the closing dance.

## 9. Production rules

- Cast ids are fixed for every video: `bn_mooka`, `bobo`, `tutu`, `zizi`, plus `baaaana_crowd` with a per-video seed.
- Nothing in one video may depend on another video (no "last time", no callbacks that need prior viewing).
- All Baaaana catalog entries carry `"series": "baaaana"`. Never edit Doopi's presets for Baaaana work.
- New renderer looks (sprout physics, livelier eyes, squash and stretch) are opt-in per character.
- Files: plan and bible in `series/baaaana/`; scripts in `scripts/series/baaaana/<video-slug>/` (one file per segment, for example `picnic_day/`); renders in `out/series/baaaana/<video-slug>/`.
- Every video is checked against section 3's originality rules before its final render.
