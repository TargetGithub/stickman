## Where more characters can come from

- **Quaternius** (quaternius.com): CC0 packs of rigged, animated low-poly characters (Ultimate Animated Characters, animals). glTF/FBX. Best fit for this style.
- **Kenney** (kenney.nl): CC0 "Animated Characters" and "Mini Characters" packs; simple, toy-like, glTF.
- **Mixamo**: free animations, but its licence forbids redistributing the raw files; fine for rendered video, not for a repo or a catalog pack. Avoid in the catalog.
- Sketchfab models are licence-per-model (many CC-BY, needing on-screen or description credit; some NC, unusable on a monetised channel). Check each one.
