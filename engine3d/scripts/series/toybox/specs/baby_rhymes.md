# Toy Box Friends: Baby & Toddler Rhymes (br01–br20)

Twenty sing-along rhyme videos for babies and toddlers (1–3 years, `audience: "babies"`), for the YouTube
channel Toybox Elementary, playlist **PreK Videos** (`PLc6zxJ3eah1I`). Each is 3–4 minutes, made with engine3d.
Sridhar reviews each video before the next set is rendered, and approves every upload in chat.

## Lyrics rule

Only **public-domain traditional rhymes** (published before 1929, or folk songs with no author) or **original
lyrics** written for this series. No modern copyrighted songs (not "Wheels on the Bus", "Baby Shark",
"Five Little Monkeys" or "If You're Happy and You Know It": their authorship or dates are uncertain). Where a
traditional rhyme has a dark or dated line, we use a gentle original verse instead (marked *original verse*).
Tunes: the traditional melody when it is public domain, otherwise an original tune (`tune` on `say`).

## Format every video follows (retention rules from the channel data)

1. **Hook in 0–5 s**: no backstory. The first line is sung or is a question with an instant payoff
   ("Who's hiding in the barn? Listen!" → doors open by 0:05).
2. **An interaction every 10–15 s**: call-and-response ("Your turn! Say moo!"), a 3-2-1 countdown with
   ticks, "Point to it!" with blinking choices, guess-the-sound, peekaboo reveals.
3. **Pop icons on key words**: a HUD word bubble or icon pops with a sound on each taught word
   (MOO!, RED, 3), characters keep moving, the camera changes angle each verse.
4. **Progress tracker** in the HUD (a row of slots, cars or stars) that fills one item per verse, with the
   last slot teased early ("A big surprise in the barn at the end!").
5. **Sung verses**: the toddler Robo voice says each line in its normal voice while a toy glockenspiel plays
   the melody note on every syllable (`tune`), quietly under the voice. (Beat chanting, `rhythm: true`, sounded
   choppy and is not used.)
6. **Recap quiz** at the end ("Which one says oink? Point to it! 3, 2, 1!"), praise, a last chorus, bye-bye.

Shared cast: **Robo** (host, the toddler voice, `meta.host`), plus the animals or friends each rhyme needs.

## Production order

br01 first (render, then Sridhar's review). Then rhymes in sets, each set starting only after Sridhar confirms
the previous one. Assets built for one rhyme go into the catalog for the others (farm stage, HUD trackers,
word bubbles, the toddler voice, `tune`).
