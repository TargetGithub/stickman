# DumpyDoo: where things stand (17 Nov 2026)

A summary for planning a YouTube channel around DumpyDoo, taken from the repo and `out/`.

## Origin and evolution (4–5 Nov 2026)

- **Fluffy** (`443c4be`, 4 Nov) came first from the same reference-image work: a round cream plush with shell fur
  (`runtime/fur.js`) in seven looks (Yogurt Man, Dumpling Buddy, Smart, Magic, Sporty, Super Fluffy). Model
  `catalog/models/fluffy.json`; one 1080p30 demo (24 s) and a model sheet in `out/demos/3d/fluffy/`.
- **Bao Buddy** (`ab98454`, 4 Nov): a smooth steamed-bun mascot with pleats, a top knot and a leaf collar
  (`runtime/dough.js`, `tools/gen_bao_buddy.mjs`).
- **Reference matching** (`d3e8e52`, `82ab00a`): body, face and collar redone against the reference picture.
- **Minion-style rounds** (5 Nov, `28f5292` → `0b4acfc`): long curvy arms and moving eyes, then its own grin, a
  real 3D talking mouth (`d76e912`), black rubber gloves, overalls with arm holes, no leaf flaps, a pink top knot,
  elbows, longer legs and shoes.
- **Renamed DumpyDoo** (`cf1a9ba`, 5 Nov): catalog id `dumpydoo`, smooth two-segment elbows, a motion test scene.

## What he can do today

- **Model:** `catalog/models/dumpydoo.json` (generated by `tools/gen_dumpydoo.mjs`; edit the generator, not the
  JSON). Named parts: `arm`/`forearm` (mirrored), `leg_left`, `leg_right`, `collar`, `jeans`, `knot`, `belly`,
  `nose`, `cheeks`. Gloves can be removed by recolouring.
- **Clothes:** `runtime/bao_clothes.js`: Minion-style denim overalls (pants, bib, back panel, straps, bib pocket,
  gold stitching, twill texture), open at the sides for the arms.
- **Mouth:** `runtime/toon_mouth.js`: a 3D mouth with lips, cavity, teeth and tongue; jaw-driven, vowel shapes
  ("oo", "ah", "ee") from lip sync.
- **Faces:** moving eyes and expressions happy, joyful, sad and surprised (see `out/demos/3d/bao_buddy/` stills).
- **Motions** (`scenes/dumpydoo_motions.js`): wave, walk, hops, spin, dance, look around, eyes, feelings, tummy
  pat, talk, point, sleep and wake, goodbye. The silent movie adds waddle, sneak, tip hat, twirl, squash, sit,
  dizzy, shrug and a curtain call (`scenes/silent/kit.js`).
- **Voice:** the `toddler` timbre, pitch 1.25 for DumpyDoo (1.1 for the storyteller), with lip sync.
- **Tests:** `dough`, `bao_clothes`, `toon_mouth` and `silent_movie` unit tests: 22 pass.

**Limits and gaps**
- The motions are helper functions inside scene files (`dumpydoo_motions.js` and `silent/kit.js`), not catalog
  actions, so a new episode has to import or copy them.
- The model description still mentions the leaf collar; check it matches the current look.
- He has no supporting cast of his own yet (only "DumpyDee" is named in one line of the movie).

## Content made

**"The Silly Day"** (`SILENT_MOVIE.md`; `ca15952`, `3ba91b4`, `f144493`): a silent-movie-style slapstick short,
about 10 minutes, with 15 chapter gags plus an opening and finale. It has an old-film look, a bowler hat and cane,
baby-voice narration and lines, a music mood per chapter, and chapter titles over each live scene.
